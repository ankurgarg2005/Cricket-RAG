CRICKET BASICS

Cricket is a bat-and-ball sport played between two teams. Each team usually has eleven players. One team bats to score runs, while the other team bowls and fields to restrict runs and dismiss batters.

CRICKET FORMATS

Cricket has three formats: Test matches, One day matches, and T20 matches

Test cricket is an international format that can last up to five days. Each team can have two innings in a standard Test match.

One Day International cricket, also called ODI cricket, generally allows each team a maximum of 50 overs in an innings.

Twenty20 cricket, also called T20 cricket, generally allows each team a maximum of 20 overs in an innings.

PLAYERS AND THEIR ROLES

A batter tries to score runs by hitting the ball. A bowler delivers the ball to try to dismiss a batter or restrict scoring. Fielders try to stop runs and catch the ball. A wicketkeeper stands behind the wickets and specializes in collecting deliveries and attempting stumpings.

CRICKET SCORING

A run is the basic unit of scoring in cricket. Batters can score runs by running between the wickets or hitting boundaries. A boundary that reaches the edge of the playing field after bouncing usually scores four runs. A ball that crosses the boundary without bouncing usually scores six runs.

OVERS AND WICKETS

An over normally consists of six valid deliveries. Wides and no-balls do not count as valid deliveries in the over. A wicket can refer to a batter being dismissed or to the set of three stumps and two bails.

COMMON CRICKET TERMS

A century is a score of 100 runs or more by a batter in an innings. A half-century is a score of 50 runs or more. A hat-trick occurs when a bowler takes wickets with three consecutive deliveries, according to the applicable match rules.

FAMOUS CRICKETERS

Sachin Tendulkar is a former Indian international cricketer widely known for his batting achievements. Virat Kohli is an Indian international cricketer known for batting. M. S. Dhoni is a former Indian captain known for wicketkeeping and leadership.

CRICKET EQUIPMENT

Common cricket equipment includes a bat, ball, wickets, protective pads, batting gloves, and a helmet. Players use protective equipment according to their role and the conditions of play.
